# Rules for the Quiz

There are a total of 26 Questions in the quiz.
All questions are scorable except Q 13
There will be a team leader for each team.
The team leader can only answer the questions.

Questions are of two types:

Type 1:
You can answer the question in 2 attempts.
Correct answer will give you 10 points.
Each wrong answer will deduct 3 points.

Type 2:
You can answer the question in 1 attempt.
Correct answer will give you 10 points.
Wrong answer will deduct 5 points.

You have to write down your answers and the leader will submit it to the quiz master.
The quiz master will check the answers and give you the score after each question.
Shouting out the answer will cost you 5 points.
